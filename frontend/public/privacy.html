Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Swish LLC ("Swish," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Swish mobile app and website (together, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

Information we collect

We collect information directly from you, automatically through your use of the Service, and from third parties who help us operate it.

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, password (stored as a one-way cryptographic hash, never in readable form), and, if you provide it, your phone number and profile photo. If you sign up with Google or Sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email from that provider.

Shipping and order information. If you buy or sell on Swish, we collect shipping addresses, order details, and purchase history.

Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. Swish does not receive or store your full card number. We retain a record of each transaction (amount, status, and a reference to the Stripe charge) for order history, receipts, taxes, and dispute handling.

Seller information. If you apply to sell on Swish, we collect the business information you provide in your seller application and, for payouts, your bank account holder name, account number, bank name, country, and currency.

Content you create. This includes live video you broadcast as a seller, live chat messages, bids and offers, and reviews you write. Live chat messages are retained for a limited time to support moderation, reporting, and dispute resolution.

Communications. If you contact support or respond to a review, we keep that correspondence to help resolve your request.

Device and usage information. We collect device push notification tokens (so we can deliver notifications you've enabled), basic device and app version information, and diagnostic data such as crash reports and error logs, which help us find and fix problems.

We do not collect your precise location, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers in the Service.

How we use information

  • Operate the Service: create and manage your account, process orders and payments, run live streams and chat, and deliver notifications.
  • Trust and safety: review reports, enforce blocks between users, moderate content, and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Support: respond to questions and resolve issues you raise with us.
  • Improve the Service: diagnose and fix bugs, and understand how features are used.
  • Comply with the law: meet tax, accounting, and recordkeeping obligations.

How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

  • Stripe, to process payments and seller payouts.
  • Amazon Web Services, which hosts our servers, stores uploaded files (such as profile photos and product images), and powers live video streaming.
  • Email and push notification providers, to deliver the messages you've asked for or that are required for account security.
  • Other users, in the ordinary course of using Swish: your display name and profile photo are visible to people you interact with, and content you post (chat messages, reviews) is visible to viewers of that stream or product.
  • Law enforcement or other parties, if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Swish or our users.

We do not sell your personal information.

Reporting and blocking

You can report a chat message or a review you believe is abusive, and you can block another user so their chat messages are no longer shown to you. Reports are reviewed by our team. Blocking does not notify the blocked user.

Account deletion and data retention

You can request account deletion at any time from Account settings in the app. Once requested, your account is signed out everywhere immediately, and deletion is scheduled for 14 days later, a window you can cancel at any time by signing back in. When deletion completes, your name, email, phone number, profile photo, and password are permanently erased. Past orders, payments, and reviews are kept on record, as required for receipts, taxes, and dispute resolution, but with nothing identifying you left on them.

Your choices

  • You can review and update your account information at any time in the app.
  • You can control notification and visibility preferences in Settings.
  • You can request deletion of your account as described above.
  • You can contact us using the information below with any other privacy question or request.

Children's privacy

Swish is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit and hashed password storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International users

Swish is operated from the United States. If you use the Service from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise any of the choices above, contact us at privacy@swishfy.com.
